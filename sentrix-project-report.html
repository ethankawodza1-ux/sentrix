Executive Summary

SENTRIX is an autonomous cybersecurity incident response pipeline that detects a cyberattack, assesses its multi-dimensional risk, enriches findings with live threat intelligence and MITRE ATT&CK context, and then intelligently decides whether to contain the threat immediately or preserve critical digital evidence first — before executing a complete, prioritised response playbook. The system models four competing dimensions — threat severity, evidence volatility, attacker activity, and asset criticality — and applies feed-driven and ATT&CK-driven score boosts to produce decisions grounded in real-world forensic and incident-response best practices.

Response Decision Matrix

🔴 CONTAIN IMMEDIATELY
CRITICAL threat + activity ≥85  |  Lateral movement active  |  Ransomware encrypting  |  CRITICAL asset under HIGH+ threat
🟡 PRESERVE EVIDENCE FIRST
Evidence volatility ≥80 + threat ≥65  |  APT / Zero-Day / Insider + volatility ≥70. Short capture window before containment.
🔵 MONITOR & ALERT
LOW or INFORMATIONAL threat level  |  Composite score <50. Increase sensor fidelity; do not disrupt operations yet.
🟣 ESCALATE TO HUMAN
Confidence <60%  |  Ambiguous profile (DDoS, Insider Threat). Legal or HR factors require human judgement before action.
